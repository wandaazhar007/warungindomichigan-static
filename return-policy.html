Return Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Food & Grocery Items Are Non-Returnable

Due to food safety regulations and FDA guidelines, most food and grocery products cannot be returned once they have been delivered. This applies to:

  • Instant noodles (mie instan), rice, and other dry goods
  • Seasonings, spices, and cooking sauces (bumbu, sambal, kecap, saus)
  • Beverages and coffee
  • Snacks and crackers (kerupuk, snack kemasan)
  • Baking ingredients and dairy products
  • Health products, vitamins, and personal care items
  • Any product where the packaging seal has been broken or opened

This is standard practice for food retailers in the United States and is required for consumer safety. We appreciate your understanding.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged, spoiled, or you received the wrong item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery via WhatsApp or email with photos of the item and packaging. We will review each case individually and work with you toward a fair resolution (replacement, store credit, or refund), at our discretion.
